# Kritika Prasai

**Ph.D. Candidate, Computer Science · University of Missouri**  
Deep Learning + Genomics, Bioinformatics, Protein Mutation fitness

---

> I build data-driven models at the intersection of machine learning and infectious disease — turning complex biological sequences into actionable insights for pandemic preparedness and vaccine design.

I apply **protein language models** and **deep learning** to problems in genomics, focusing on influenza and emerging viruses — from raw sequencing data to interpretable models that inform real-world decisions.

---

## Research

**Pandemic risk prediction**  
Multi-task deep learning for SARS-CoV-2 ACE2–RBD binding, using ESM-2 embeddings and cross-attention to capture residue-level interactions.

**Influenza evolution & vaccines**  
Large-scale genomic analyses of H3N2 antigenic evolution; integrated genomic + scRNA-seq data to trace immune escape patterns.

**Transcriptomics & expression**  
RNA-seq pipelines using GLMs and quasi-likelihood tests; visualization of gene networks and enrichment from multi-omics data.
